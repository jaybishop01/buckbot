BuckBot

A personal finance tool, built and used by one person.

What it does

BuckBot connects to its owner's own bank, card, and loan accounts through Plaid. It reads balances and transactions, sorts spending into categories, and shows a cash forecast. It is read-only: it cannot move money.

Who uses it

One person, the owner. BuckBot is not a product or a service. It has no customers, no sign-up, and no other users.

Privacy

  • Account data is stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • Data is never sold, shared, or given to anyone else.
  • Bank sign-in details are entered with the bank or Plaid, and are never seen or stored by BuckBot.
  • A connection can be removed at any time, and its data deleted.
